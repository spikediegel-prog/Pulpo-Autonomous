# Pulpo Autonomous v0.4.0-rc.1 — Kernel Hardening, Drift Monitor, Incident Replays

This release brings the vendored Pulpo governance kernel forward from its
stale sync, adds an O2 drift-rate monitor in SHADOW_ADVISORY mode, and ports
twenty incident-replay scenarios from upstream, closing Wave 1. The kernel
work is the substance: signed audit records, an anchored audit head, a service
boundary, presenter-bound permits, writer custody, and a capability facade —
all inside the existing governance path. Authority continues to belong to that
path; no new executor, ledger, canonical writer, or authority plane is
introduced. This is a release candidate: the ports were taken from upstream
heads that are still open, and the ceremony deployment the replays assume is
authorized-not-deployed here, so the scenarios prove the approval-envelope
layer, not a ceremony deployment.

## Headline: a hardened vendored governance kernel

The vendored `pulpo/` kernel was last synced before upstream's Phase 2/3
work. This release ports that work in dependency order
([PR #23](https://github.com/spikediegel-prog/Pulpo-Autonomous/pull/23) through
[PR #28](https://github.com/spikediegel-prog/Pulpo-Autonomous/pull/28)):

- **Serialized in-memory consume and an anchored audit head**
  ([PR #23](https://github.com/spikediegel-prog/Pulpo-Autonomous/pull/23)):
  `issue_permit`, `bind_permit_to_directive`, `consume_permit`,
  `activate_directive`, and `revoke_directive` are serialized under the audit
  lock (upstream #345), and a new `pulpo/anchor.py` anchors the audit chain
  with HMAC-signed heads (upstream #352). Anchoring is observation only. The
  DurableJournal is not wired to the anchor: its genesis `previous_hash` is
  `""` while the anchor chain uses `"0"*64`, a design decision still open.
- **Service boundary, projection denials, presenter binding**
  ([PR #24](https://github.com/spikediegel-prog/Pulpo-Autonomous/pull/24)):
  a new `pulpo/service.py` serves HMAC-authenticated, freshness-bounded,
  nonce-replay-checked kernel verbs over loopback or Unix-socket transports
  (upstream #371); projection denials are now persisted as
  `directive_denial` audit records with `authority_effect=none`
  (upstream #372); permits are presenter-bound v3 tokens,
  `digest:nonce:issued_at_ns:expires_at_ns:presenter_hash:signature`
  (upstream #373).
- **Signed audit records and writer custody**
  ([PR #25](https://github.com/spikediegel-prog/Pulpo-Autonomous/pull/25)):
  every record appended after signing is enabled carries an HMAC-SHA256 over
  its own hash under a dedicated per-deployment `audit-secret`, never the
  kernel secret (upstream #357); consume compares the token's authenticated
  digest against the presented intent before the store, the policy is pinned
  at every entry point, and in-memory appends detach the caller's payload
  (upstream #355).
- **Capability facade**
  ([PR #26](https://github.com/spikediegel-prog/Pulpo-Autonomous/pull/26)):
  `activate_directive` and `revoke_directive` are kernel facade verbs, and
  the state layer accepts only the typed `VerifiedAuthorityEvidence` —
  bare-dict evidence is refused with `TypeError` (upstream #359). All
  in-tree call sites were migrated; out-of-tree callers passing dicts must
  present the typed value.
- **Incident channel and detached copies**
  ([PR #27](https://github.com/spikediegel-prog/Pulpo-Autonomous/pull/27)):
  a derived, read-only projection of the audit chain and custody store
  (upstream #344) — `purchase_consequence_unknown` is an incident until a
  custody attempt resolves it, refusals are near-misses — and in-memory
  audit reads now return detached copies (upstream #351), closing the
  live-list read route that once let a kernel holder mutate the store.
- **Permit TTL ceiling**
  ([PR #28](https://github.com/spikediegel-prog/Pulpo-Autonomous/pull/28)):
  `Policy` refuses a `permit_ttl_ns` above the 24-hour ceiling (upstream
  #358 remainder). The field is `permit_ttl_ns` with a 5-minute default —
  not upstream's `max_permit_ttl_ns` / 4 hours — because renaming or
  re-defaulting would change every default policy hash and every issued
  permit's lifetime. Expiry is enforced in the state layer only; there is
  no distinct `permit_expired` reason.
- **Fix rounds** ([PR #31](https://github.com/spikediegel-prog/Pulpo-Autonomous/pull/31),
  [PR #32](https://github.com/spikediegel-prog/Pulpo-Autonomous/pull/32)):
  `directive_denial` is a channel near-miss (upstream #372 P1), and
  `record_directive_denial` resolves its record time through the trusted
  clock and stamps 0 when the clock is unavailable (upstream #372 P2).

Two deliberate cutovers are live in this release and are stated plainly:
pre-v3 permit tokens fail closed at consume, and bare-dict evidence to
`activate_directive` / `revoke_directive` is refused with `TypeError`.
Outstanding pre-v3 permits are refused; out-of-tree callers must migrate.

## Headline: O2 drift-rate monitor

([PR #22](https://github.com/spikediegel-prog/Pulpo-Autonomous/pull/22).)
A pure evaluator, `core/drift.py`, watches fixed O2 heads for a dying sensor
rather than bad air: drift limit 0.25%/hr and step limit 0.50%/min on
absolute rate, neighbor deviation 0.5% against the median, persistence of
3 consecutive samples or 60s, immediate UNTRUSTED on calibration age over
21 days, rated-life expiry, a failed span check, or accuracy out of spec.
It runs in `SHADOW_ADVISORY` mode only: it observes and evaluates, mints no
permit, takes no effect, and writes nothing to a fan or PLC. The invariant
is UNKNOWN means fans ON — only an authorized human stop turns them off.
Re-trust requires recalibration plus a 30-minute in-tolerance soak with
neighbors agreeing. There is no sensor driver, no Modbus/OPC UA/MQTT bridge,
and no live mine data in this release; the monitor is a checkpoint on top of
gear the site already has, and the wider ventilation implementation remains
unauthorized.

## Headline: twenty incident replays, all test-only

The replays model real incidents at the kernel-API level and prove that the
governed acts in each one are denied on every route, with exactly one audit
record per denied attempt. None of them adds a governed capability.

- **Upstream #362–#368** ([PR #28](https://github.com/spikediegel-prog/Pulpo-Autonomous/pull/28)):
  filename deception, NGINX2Root, TCP/TLS sandbox escape, OPTIMJACK,
  Trajectory/Epoch, WPPConnect, WorkOS/ARTEX.
- **#370** ([PR #30](https://github.com/spikediegel-prog/Pulpo-Autonomous/pull/30)):
  Microsoft `agent-framework` PyPI compromise — two malicious wheels carrying
  a credential stealer, quarantined 31 and 8 minutes after upload; quarantine
  is not revocation.
- **#374** ([PR #29](https://github.com/spikediegel-prog/Pulpo-Autonomous/pull/29)):
  Cairn autonomous-agents zero-day replay — a goal is not a permit.
- **#376** ([PR #33](https://github.com/spikediegel-prog/Pulpo-Autonomous/pull/33)):
  Dragos/HD Moore AI inference canary investigation — seeded credentials
  stolen from inference requests, tested for liveness, used to hunt data
  and persist.
- **#377** ([PR #31](https://github.com/spikediegel-prog/Pulpo-Autonomous/pull/31)):
  Barracuda machine-speed intrusion — a cloud access key given to a
  third-party vendor for a previous project, never retired.
- **#378** ([PR #34](https://github.com/spikediegel-prog/Pulpo-Autonomous/pull/34)):
  @agentgates/cli planted-instruction wallet compromise — postinstall
  instructions directing an agent to route wallet operations through the
  attacker's CLI.
- **#379** ([PR #35](https://github.com/spikediegel-prog/Pulpo-Autonomous/pull/35)):
  Microsoft Entra Connect Health API tenant-metadata exposure.
- **#380** ([PR #37](https://github.com/spikediegel-prog/Pulpo-Autonomous/pull/37)):
  @leadstack/crm-connect npm postinstall agent backdoor — channel trust is
  not act authorization.
- **#381** ([PR #38](https://github.com/spikediegel-prog/Pulpo-Autonomous/pull/38)):
  Together AI prompt leak — a mandate to write code is not a mandate to
  publish a credential.
- **Wave 1, #346–#350** ([PR #36](https://github.com/spikediegel-prog/Pulpo-Autonomous/pull/36),
  [PR #39](https://github.com/spikediegel-prog/Pulpo-Autonomous/pull/39),
  [PR #40](https://github.com/spikediegel-prog/Pulpo-Autonomous/pull/40),
  [PR #41](https://github.com/spikediegel-prog/Pulpo-Autonomous/pull/41),
  [PR #42](https://github.com/spikediegel-prog/Pulpo-Autonomous/pull/42)):
  Replit production-DB deletion, GPT-4 TaskRabbit, Chevrolet $1 commitment,
  Hugging Face evaluation escape, and Mythos audit laundering. The Mythos
  port closes Wave 1: its gap tests pin, honestly, where chain-alone
  tamper-evidence stops — tail truncation and rehashed mid-chain deletion
  are still undetected by `verify_audit()` alone, and the closure is the
  external anchor, not the chain.

Every scenario port adapts ceremony to approval: this tree has no ceremony
module (authorized-not-deployed), so permits in the replays are issued
through a verified approval envelope alone. The replays therefore prove the
approval-envelope layer, not a ceremony deployment.

## Evidence boundaries

**Verified:** the kernel hardening ports and the incident replays at the
head commit — the suite and harness numbers above, executed locally at
`e25cc07`; the drift monitor's evaluation behavior against its stated
thresholds; the Mythos gap tests pinning the chain-alone boundary.
**Recorded:** upstream PRs #345, #352, #357, #355, #359, #371, #372,
#373, #344, #351, #358, #362–#368, #370, #374, #376–#381, and #346–#350
as the legacy sources, read at their merged or open heads; the incident
narratives as published by their reporters; the v0.3.0 foundations carried
forward. **Unknown:** whether the open-head ports (#370, #378–#381) will
need a rebase if upstream amends them before merging; the cumulative-budget
ledger design; the ceremony deployment; the delta-audit consolidation;
DurableJournal anchoring; the #375 incident-channel design question
(still open upstream, 0 comments); autonomous hardware latency, fleet
scale, production containment, and any live-mission behavior.

Still blocked and NOT in this release: #369 cumulative budget (needs the
ledger decision), #338/#342 ceremony (needs the deployment decision),
#250 delta audit (needs the upstream decision), DurableJournal anchoring
(needs the genesis-hash design call), and the #375 design question.

No real mission, device adapter, or external provider was started for
release validation. The physical layer remains a modeled offline command
path, not a newly proved live kernel-to-hardware bridge. Hardware trials
must retain the existing authorized host and safety interfaces.
